Coratina

Born in Corato, cradled by the limestone-rich Murge plateau, this distinguished cultivar yields an oil of extraordinary character—robust and peppery, with a lingering finish that speaks of ancient groves and centuries of mastery. Rich in polyphenols, each drop carries the essence of Apulia's sun-drenched hills and the passage of civilizations.
